Simple VoIP

What is this?
This is a simple voice calling app that works over the internet - like a mini version of WhatsApp or Discord calls.

Why does it exist?
It was built to demonstrate how real-time voice communication works behind the scenes in computer networks.

How does it work?
- When you click “Connect”, your browser talks to the server.
- When you click “Start Call”, your voice is sent directly to your peer using fast UDP packets.
- Everything happens in real time — no phone numbers, no accounts, just pure network magic!
